SCRIB3D P1 3D Printing Pen with Display

Check price 4.4(24,467)

About this product

Most 3D printing happens inside a machine. This pen puts it in your hand: plug it in, let it heat up, and you draw and build objects in three dimensions with PLA plastic filament. The P1 has a display and a stepless speed slider for anything from slow, intricate detail to faster infill, and its temperature is adjustable for PLA (160 to 210°C) or ABS (210 to 235°C) filament. A ceramic nozzle and a 2-minute auto sleep are built in, and warm-up takes about 2 to 3 minutes. The kit comes ready to use, with the 3D pen, three starter colors of PLA filament (30 feet in total), a stencil book with project guide, and a power adapter. The listing pitches it as a family and hobbyist gift, and for making handmade gifts and decorations.

Why We Like It

It flips 3D printing from something a machine does into something you do with your hand. A pen, a coil of filament, and a stencil book are all it takes to start drawing in three dimensions.

Highlights

  • Draw and build in three dimensions by hand, with PLA filament
  • Display and a stepless speed slider for detailed or fast work
  • Adjustable temperature for PLA (160-210°C) and ABS (210-235°C) filament
  • Ceramic nozzle and a 2-minute auto sleep
  • Kit includes 3 starter PLA colors (30 ft), a stencil book with project guide, and a power adapter
