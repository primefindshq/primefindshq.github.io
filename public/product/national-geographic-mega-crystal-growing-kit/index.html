About this product

This National Geographic kit from Blue Marble grows six crystals in blue, red, purple, green, yellow and glow-in-the-dark, with crystals forming in about 3-4 days, per the listing. Two silicone growing chambers make the process easier. A USB-powered light-up stand (cable included) displays five finished crystals. It also includes four real specimens (rose quartz, fluorite, blue calcite and a quartz geode) and a full-color learning guide. It's an Amazon exclusive for ages 8 and up.
