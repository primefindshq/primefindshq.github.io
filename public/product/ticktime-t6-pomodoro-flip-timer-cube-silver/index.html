Ticktime T6 Pomodoro Flip Timer Cube, Silver

$37.99 4.3(2,474)

About this product

The Ticktime T6 is a small rechargeable timer cube with a built-in gyroscope: flip it onto a side to instantly start a preset interval of 3, 5, 10, 15, 25, 45 or 60 minutes, per the listing. It has a preset Pomodoro cycle (25 minutes of focus, 5 minutes of rest), custom countdowns from 1 second to 99 minutes 59 seconds, a stopwatch mode, and pause and resume. Alerts can be sound, vibration or silent flash. It has an LED display and charges over USB-C. It measures about 1.9 x 1.7 x 1.8 in. This is the silver version.

Why We Like It

Flipping a physical cube is a much stronger 'start working' signal than opening a timer app on the phone that distracts you. It's as useful in the kitchen as on the desk.

Highlights

  • 25/5 Pomodoro cycle
  • Custom timer up to 99:59 + stopwatch
  • Sound, vibration or silent alerts
