About this product

The HIWARE teapot is hand-crafted from heat-resistant borosilicate glass with a non-drip spout and ergonomic handle, per the listing. A removable 18/10 stainless steel mesh infuser handles loose-leaf and blooming teas, and the lid fits with or without the basket. It can go on gas or electric stovetops at medium heat or in the microwave, and holds 1000 ml (about 33 oz).

Why We Like It

Watching loose leaf or blooming tea unfurl in clear glass is part of the fun, and it's a pretty gift for any tea lover.
