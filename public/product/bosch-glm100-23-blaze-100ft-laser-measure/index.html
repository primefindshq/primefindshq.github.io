About this product

The Bosch GLM100-23 Blaze measures distances up to 100 ft to within 1/16 inch, displaying results in meters, feet or inches with fractions or decimals, per the listing. It uses two buttons (one to measure, one to round results from 1/2 in. to 1/32 in.) and has a backlit display with large icons. It's pocket-sized, weighs about 86 g and uses a Class II laser. Two AA batteries are included.

Why We Like It

Measuring a room alone with a tape measure is a two-person job; this makes it a one-click job. Handy for moving, furniture shopping and DIY.
