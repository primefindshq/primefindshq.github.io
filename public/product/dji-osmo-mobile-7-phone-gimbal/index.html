DJI Osmo Mobile 7 Phone Gimbal

$75.00 4.4(4,897)

About this product

The Osmo Mobile 7 stabilizes a phone on three axes for smoother video. It has an integrated foldable design with a built-in tripod, weighs about 300 g, and has an anti-slip handle. With the DJI Mimo app it supports ActiveTrack 7.0 subject tracking, ShotGuides and One-Tap Edit. The listing gives a maximum operating time of 10 hours, and it can charge a phone over USB-C. Folded, it measures 179 x 95 x 39 mm.

Why We Like It

It takes shaky phone footage and makes it look deliberate, and the built-in tripod means one device covers vlogging, travel clips and group shots. It folds small enough for a daypack.
