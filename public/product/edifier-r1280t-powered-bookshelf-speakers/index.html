About this product

The Edifier R1280T pairs a 13mm silk dome tweeter with a 4-inch full-range driver in each speaker, per the listing, for 42W RMS total. Two inputs (3.5mm and RCA) let you connect two devices at once, side-panel knobs adjust bass and treble, and a remote controls volume. The cabinets are MDF. This is the black version.

Why We Like It

They're a big step up from laptop or TV speakers, and a classic choice for a desk or record-player setup.
