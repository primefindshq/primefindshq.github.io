WSTER Cyberpunk Truck Bluetooth Speaker

About this product

The WSTER WS-580L is a Bluetooth speaker built into a geometric, pickup-truck-shaped body, with movable wheels, a front LED light strip, RGB wheel lighting and a small color display screen. It connects over Bluetooth 5.4 and also plays via FM radio, USB, a TF card or an AUX cable, has a built-in microphone for hands-free calls, and can pair with a second WS-580L unit over TWS for wider stereo sound. The listing also mentions a remote selfie control function. Output is listed at 15 watts.

Why We Like It

It's more desk display than plain speaker: RGB wheels, a light strip and a little screen, shaped like a truck that happens to play music. A playful pick for a gaming or cyberpunk-styled setup.
