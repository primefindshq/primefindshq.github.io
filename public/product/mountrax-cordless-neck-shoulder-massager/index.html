About this product

The MOUNTRAX massager drapes over the neck and shoulders with wide arm sleeves your forearms rest inside, so your hands stay free to type, hold a book or a cup while it runs. It uses a kneading motion the listing describes as 6D shiatsu, with 2 kneading modes, 2 intensity settings and 2 heat levels, plus a 10-minute auto shutoff. It's cordless and rechargeable, listed at about 2.5 hours of use on a full charge (about 3.5 hours to charge). The listing also describes using it on the upper back, lower back, waist or legs, and the fabric cover is removable and washable. It measures about 14.1 x 12.9 x 6.7 in and weighs about 2.6 lb.

Why We Like It

The wide, hands-free arm sleeves are the distinctive part: it drapes on and stays put, so it doesn't need to be held in place like a lot of neck massagers do, which makes it easier to keep using while doing something else.

  • 10-minute auto shutoff
  • Removable, washable cover
