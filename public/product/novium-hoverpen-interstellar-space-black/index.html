novium Hoverpen Interstellar, Space Black Magnetic Hovering Ballpoint Pen

Check price 4.6(2,482)

About this product

Wait, how is that floating? The novium Hoverpen Interstellar is a working ballpoint pen that hangs at a 23.5-degree angle in a ring of magnetic repulsion above its pedestal, no power source needed. Give it a twist and it spins; the listing says a spin can last up to 20 seconds. The body is machined from a single piece of aircraft-grade aluminum, the set is listed in aluminum and zinc alloy, and the finish is Space Black with a black pedestal and cap ring. The cap snaps onto the pen magnetically. It writes with a Schmidt ballpoint cartridge in black ink (0.8 mm line), and it is refillable: pull the cartridge with the included pincher tool and drop in any D1-size refill. The listing gives the pen as 5.65 inches long and 10 mm in diameter, with a listed weight of 218 g. Listed contents: the Hoverpen, an ink cartridge, an instruction guide and the pedestal, packed in a gift-ready box.

Why We Like It

It is an ordinary pen doing something a pen should not: suspended at an angle above a sculpted pedestal, no strings. On a desk it is a piece of small, dark, futuristic hardware that also signs your name.

Highlights

  • Hovers at 23.5 degrees above a magnetic pedestal and can be spun
  • Aluminum and zinc alloy, Space Black finish; body machined from a single piece of aluminum
  • Magnetic cap; Schmidt ballpoint cartridge, black ink, 0.8 mm
  • Refillable with any D1-size refill using the included pincher tool
  • Gift-ready box with pen, pedestal, ink cartridge and instructions
