ATuMan Mooda Smart Thermometer Hygrometer Clock

Check price 3.4(11)

About this product

The ATuMan Mooda reads room temperature and humidity and shows them on a retro pixel screen, alongside a second small pixel display with an animated character whose expression is meant to react to conditions. A mechanical joystick lets you flick through different pixel expressions and views. The listing gives a temperature accuracy of about ±2°C over 14.2°F-140°F, and humidity accuracy of about ±7% RH over 0-99% RH, with readings refreshing every 30 seconds. It has an 800mAh rechargeable battery charged over USB, measures about 6.1 x 2.7 x 1.1 in, and can sit on a desk or mount on a wall. The listing notes the unit runs warm while charging and recommends letting it sit for 30-60 minutes after a full charge before relying on its readings.

Why We Like It

It's a small, playful way to keep an eye on a room's temperature and humidity instead of a plain LCD readout, with a joystick to flip through pixel-art views. A fun desk gadget for anyone who likes retro screens.

  • Readings refresh every 30 seconds
  • 800mAh rechargeable battery via USB
  • About 6.1 x 2.7 x 1.1 in; desk or wall use
