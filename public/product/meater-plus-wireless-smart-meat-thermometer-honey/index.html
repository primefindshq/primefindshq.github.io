MEATER Plus Wireless Smart Meat Thermometer, Honey

$79.99 4.3(48,522)

About this product

The MEATER Plus is a single wireless probe that sits in the meat with no cables. It has two sensors: one for internal meat temperature (up to 212°F) and one for ambient temperature (up to 527°F), per the listing. Its bamboo charging block has a built-in Bluetooth repeater to extend range. The MEATER app gives step-by-step guided cooks, alerts and estimated finish times. A 5-minute charge gives about 2 hours of cook time, and the probe is dishwasher safe. The box includes the probe, the bamboo charger and a AAA battery.

Why We Like It

No wire snaking out of the oven or grill lid, and a phone alert instead of guessing. It's one of those gadgets people buy once and then give to everyone who grills.
