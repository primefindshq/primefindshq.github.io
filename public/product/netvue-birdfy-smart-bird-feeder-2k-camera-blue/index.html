NETVUE by Birdfy Smart Bird Feeder with 2K AI Camera, Blue

Check price 4.4(1,538)

About this product

Put a feeder outside and get a live feed of who shows up. The NETVUE by Birdfy Feeder (Birdfy 1 Series) holds 1.5 liters of seed and has a built-in 2K AI camera that automatically records each visit. The listing says it sends notifications to your phone, lets you watch live or replay clips on a phone, tablet or computer, and has color night vision for after-dark visitors. The Birdfy app can identify species: the listing cites about 11,000 species worldwide and says advanced AI features require a subscription, while free cloud storage and sharing with 20+ members are named in the title. It connects over Wi-Fi and works with Alexa. On power, the listing describes it as solar powered, mentions a solar panel and a built-in 5200 mAh battery, and its included-components line lists only the feeder with camera, so check the listing to confirm what comes in your configuration. It is ABS plastic in Blue, 8 by 7 by 11 inches and 3 pounds, mounts on a wall, pole or tree, and is listed as weatherproof.

Why We Like It

The trick is the swap: an ordinary feeder becomes a small piece of smart-home tech, with a camera watching the perch and your phone telling you who arrived. The bright cardinal in the product shot is the whole idea.

Highlights

  • Built-in 2K AI camera with auto recording and phone notifications
  • Live view and replay clips in the Birdfy app; color night vision
  • AI species ID (about 11,000 species cited); advanced AI features need a subscription
  • 1.5 L seed capacity; Wi-Fi; works with Alexa
  • Listed as solar powered with a 5200 mAh battery; wall, pole or tree mount
