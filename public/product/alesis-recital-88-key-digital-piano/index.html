About this product

The Alesis Recital has 88 full-sized semi-weighted keys with adjustable touch response, per the listing. It offers 5 voices (acoustic piano, electric piano, organ, synth and bass) with chorus and reverb, two built-in 20W speakers and 128-note polyphony. Standard, split, layer and lesson modes help beginners, and it has a sustain pedal input (pedal not included), a headphone output and RCA outputs. It runs from the included adapter or 6 D batteries, and includes trial subscriptions to Skoove and Melodics lessons.

Why We Like It

It's a full-size keyboard at a beginner-friendly price, with headphone practice so nobody else has to listen to scales.
