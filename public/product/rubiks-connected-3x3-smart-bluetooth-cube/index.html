About this product

The Rubik's Connected is the classic Rubik's Cube with Bluetooth built in, paired to a free companion app that tracks your moves in real time. The listing describes a step-by-step academy that teaches solving the cube in 7 steps, mini-games, solve-time stats, and online battles against other cubers on a global leaderboard. Setup is charge the cube, install the app, and turn on Bluetooth; on Android, location services may also need to be enabled. It's built as a magnetic speed cube under the smart electronics.

Why We Like It

It turns the most famous puzzle in the world into something you can actually track progress on: real solve times, a guided path from never-solved-it to fast, and battles against strangers online. A satisfying gift for anyone who's always meant to learn.
