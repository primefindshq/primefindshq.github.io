About this product

In Apples to Apples, a rotating judge plays a Green Apple description card and everyone picks the Red Apple card from their hand that fits best, per the listing. The judge picks a favorite, and the first player to collect 4 Green Apple cards wins. The deck is color-blind accessible, and it's for 4 to 8 players ages 12+.
