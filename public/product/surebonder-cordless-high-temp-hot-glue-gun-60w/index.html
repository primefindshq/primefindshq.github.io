Surebonder Cordless High-Temp Hot Glue Gun, 60W

About this product

The Surebonder CL-800F is a full-size high-temperature glue gun that reaches 380°F for bonding metal, wood, ceramics, leather and other materials, per the listing. It sits on a heating base that keeps it warm, can be used for up to 3 minutes off the base cordless, or indefinitely with the 5 ft cord attached. Its 60 W capacity supports continuous glue flow. Safety features include a fuse, an insulated nozzle and side fins that stop glue backing up when the gun is laid down. A mini glue pad is included.

Why We Like It

Cordless means no cable dragging across the project, which makes crafts and quick home fixes much less fiddly.
