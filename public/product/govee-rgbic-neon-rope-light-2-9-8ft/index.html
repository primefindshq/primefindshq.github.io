Govee RGBIC Neon Rope Light 2, 9.8ft

$54.99 4.6(1,287)

About this product

Govee's second-generation neon rope light is a 9.8 ft flexible LED tube you shape with bendable mounting clips to draw lines, outlines or words on a wall. The listing lists 64 preset RGBIC lighting effects and a shape-mapping feature in the Govee Home app that uses your phone camera to recognize the shape and apply effects across its segments. It works with Matter, Alexa and Google Assistant; 5 GHz Wi-Fi is not supported. Govee says the material is 14% more flexible and softer than the previous version. For indoor use.

Why We Like It

It lets you draw with light on a wall instead of just sticking a strip behind something, which is why it shows up in so many gaming rooms and bedrooms. Shape mapping makes custom designs actually animate properly.
