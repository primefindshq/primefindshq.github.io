About this product

The Rocketbook Core is a reusable notebook: write with the included Pilot FriXion pen, scan each page into the Rocketbook app to send it to Google Drive, Dropbox, OneNote or another cloud service, then wipe the page clean with a damp cloth and start over. The listing describes Smart Titles and Smart Tags in the dot-grid pages for naming and organizing scanned files. This is the Letter size (8.5 x 11 in), 32 pages, with a scratch-resistant spiral-bound cover; an Executive size is also sold. Listed contents are the notebook, the FriXion pen and a cloth for wiping pages.

Why We Like It

It has the feel of writing on real paper with a notebook that never runs out: every page becomes a digital file, then goes blank again. A tidy, low-waste alternative to a stack of used notebooks.
