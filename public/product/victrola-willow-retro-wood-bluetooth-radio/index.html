Victrola Willow Retro Wood Bluetooth Radio

Check price 4.4(7,491)

About this product

The Victrola Willow pairs a vintage wood-finished body with modern playback: an analog AM/FM rotary dial alongside Bluetooth streaming from a phone, tablet or laptop. Built-in stereo speakers handle both the radio and streamed audio, and separate bass and treble knobs let you shape the sound; the volume knob doubles as the power switch. It measures about 9.5 x 6.3 x 4.9 in and is shown here in Espresso. The listing frames it as part of Victrola's 115-year history in audio design.

Why We Like It

It looks like a piece of 1950s furniture but streams from a phone just as easily as it tunes into FM, so it fits a desk or shelf without looking like the rest of your electronics.
