TNOIE Volcano Lamp, Erupting Lava-Bead Night Light with USB or Battery Power

Check price 4.1(613)

About this product

A miniature eruption for your desk or shelf. The TNOIE Volcano Lamp is a lava-lamp-style night light: red lava beads erupt from the top of a small volcano and cascade through the chamber, carried by a gentle current, with a soft red glow behind them. Setup is four steps: fill to the marked line, add the beads, add 3 drops of soap (the listing warns the beads will stick together without it) and power on with the switch on the base. It runs either from USB or from 3 AA batteries, which are not included, but not both at once. The listing describes the motor as whisper-quiet, under 25 dB. It measures 9.1 by 2.8 by 2.8 inches, weighs 14.4 ounces, is black ABS plastic with a red glow, and has a 1-year warranty. The listing pitches it as a gift for kids, teens, men and women, and as a desktop decoration for a room or office.

Why We Like It

It is a lava lamp with a plot: the beads burst out of the volcano crater and rain back down through a red glow. It is slim enough for a desk or shelf, and it runs off a USB port or batteries, so it goes where a socket does not.
