Ooni Karu 12 Multi-Fuel Outdoor Pizza Oven

$248.95 4.6(654)

About this product

The Karu 12 burns real wood or charcoal out of the box, and can run on gas with the Ooni Gas Burner attachment, sold separately. According to the listing it's ready in about 15 minutes, reaches 950°F, and cooks a 12-inch stone-baked pizza in around 60 seconds. It weighs 26.4 lb, and the legs fold down and the chimney comes off for transport. A redesigned fuel tray is built to maximize airflow. It includes a pizza stone.

Why We Like It

It's the step from 'homemade pizza' to actual pizzeria-style crust, in a size that fits on a patio table. A serious gift for anyone who already makes their own dough.
