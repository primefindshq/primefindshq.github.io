About this product

The WONDERBOOM 4 plays 360° sound from a compact body with up to 14 hours of battery, per the listing. It's IP67 rated (submersible in 1 m of water for 30 minutes), dustproof, drop-proof, and it floats. Outdoor Boost raises volume outside, Podcast Mode clarifies voices, and two units pair for stereo. Bluetooth range is 131 ft (40 m). It charges via USB-C (cable not included) and has a carry loop. Plastic parts include at least 31% recycled plastic. This is the blue color.

Why We Like It

It's the speaker you don't worry about: toss it in the pool, the sand or the shower. Small enough for any bag.
