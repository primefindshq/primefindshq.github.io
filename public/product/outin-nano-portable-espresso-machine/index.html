OutIn Nano Portable Espresso Machine

Check price 4.2(5,744)

About this product

The OutIn Nano heats its own water and pulls a shot with 20-bar pressure, working with either ground coffee or NS-compatible capsules. The listing says it heats 1.7 oz of room-temperature water to about 198°F in around 200 seconds, and runs on a built-in 2500mAh battery, good for about 5 hot shots from room-temperature water or 200+ shots when you start with hot water; it also charges over USB-C, including from a 10-15W car charger. It weighs about 670 g, is made from food-grade stainless steel, and the listing notes it should travel in carry-on luggage only, not used in flight. This is the Space Gray colorway.

Why We Like It

It's a real espresso shot, not instant coffee, made from a machine small enough to fit in a bag: camping, a hotel room, or a desk without a coffee machine. A genuinely surprising gadget for anyone who takes coffee seriously on the move.

Highlights

  • Self-heating; 20-bar pressure for espresso shots
  • Works with ground coffee or NS-compatible capsules
  • About 5 hot shots per charge from room-temp water (listed)
  • USB-C charging, including from a car charger
  • About 670 g; stainless steel
