Logitech MX Creative Console, Graphite

Check price 4.1(443)

About this product

Your software controls, turned into something you can touch. The Logitech MX Creative Console pairs a keypad of 9 customizable LCD display keys with a separate dialpad: an aluminum low-friction dial for precise adjustments, a roller for navigating, and extra buttons. Instead of hunting through menus, you assign tools and actions to the keys, dial and roller, and the LCD keys show what each one does. The listing says you can set up to 15 keypad pages by profile, upload your own icons and create shortcuts and Smart Actions, with recommended profiles to get started. Named integrations in the listing include Adobe Photoshop, Premiere Pro, Illustrator, Lightroom and Lightroom Classic, Final Cut Pro, Figma, DaVinci Resolve, Spotify and Zoom, and more, so it suits editors, designers, photographers, streamers and anyone building a control-heavy desk setup, whether or not they work professionally. It needs the Logi Options+ app, listed as available for recent versions of Windows and macOS. The keypad connects by USB-C, and the listing shows a stand and a USB-C to USB-C cable in the box, plus 2 AAA batteries listed for the dialpad. It carries a 1-year limited hardware warranty. The listing also mentions an included 3-month Adobe Creative Cloud All Apps membership, but it doesn't show redemption terms or regional limits, so check current eligibility before counting on it.

Why We Like It

It looks like a miniature command center: little screens that change with the app you're in, and a real dial for the adjustments that feel awkward with a mouse. It's a distinctive addition to a creator desk, and useful to anyone who likes physical controls.
