PETKIT PuraMax 2 Automatic Self-Cleaning Cat Litter Box, Basic Bundle

Check price 4.2(2,596)

About this product

Your cat still uses a litter box. You just don't have to scoop it every time. The PETKIT PuraMax 2 (P9902) is an enclosed, rotating self-cleaning litter box: after your cat leaves, it runs a cleaning cycle and moves waste into a 7L waste bin, which you still need to empty. The PETKIT app, over 2.4GHz Wi-Fi, lets you check its working status, set the cleaning mode (auto, manual, timer or empty) and see usage records such as visit frequency and duration; that is usage data, not a medical tool. Safety is built around 7 infrared sensors and 4 weight sensors that pause the unit when a cat approaches, and an anti-pinch design that keeps the entrance open. The 76L interior has a 7.87-inch low entry and is listed for cats weighing 3.3 to 22 lb; the listing says not to use it for pets under 6 months. For odor control it pairs a sealed waste bin with the N50 2.0 odor remover, which reduces odors rather than removing them entirely. It works with clumping litter, comes with a 2-year warranty, and should sit on a flat floor, not on carpet or mats. Listed contents for this basic bundle: the litter box, N50 2.0 odor eliminator, one roll of trash bags (20 pcs), decorative stickers and a user manual; the listing text also mentions extra items, so check it for the full list.

Why We Like It

It takes an unglamorous chore and turns it into a small automated system: the cat uses it, the box cleans up, waste goes into a bin, and you can check in from the app. It feels less like a pet-supply item and more like a piece of smart-home gear.

Highlights

  • PETKIT app over 2.4GHz Wi-Fi: status, cleaning modes, usage records

  • 76L interior, 7.87 in low entry; cats 3.3-22 lb, not for pets under 6 months
