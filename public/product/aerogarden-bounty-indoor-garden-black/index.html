AeroGarden Bounty, Black

Check price 4.4(1,804)

About this product

The AeroGarden Bounty grows up to 9 plants, as tall as 24 in, hydroponically: no soil, no mess, just pre-seeded pods dropped into a water reservoir. The listing lists a 3-step water level indicator, Wi-Fi connectivity and Alexa compatibility, and 40-watt energy-efficient LED grow lights that automatically turn on and off and are tuned for growth. The system reminds you when to add water and liquid plant food. Listed contents are a seed pod kit with pre-seeded pods, liquid plant food, a quick start guide and a 6-inch trellis. This is the Black colorway.

Why We Like It

It takes the guesswork out of growing herbs indoors: no soil to manage, a light that schedules itself, and reminders instead of a plant you have to remember to check on. A genuinely useful, low-effort way to have fresh herbs on a counter.
