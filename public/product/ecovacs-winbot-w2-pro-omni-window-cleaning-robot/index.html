ECOVACS WINBOT W2 PRO Omni Window Cleaning Robot

$379.00 4.1(518)

About this product

The WINBOT W2 PRO Omni is a window-cleaning robot that attaches to glass and works across it automatically using WIN-SLAM 4.0 path planning, per the listing. Three nozzles spray water ahead of a wet cleaning cloth. It can run plugged in, or on battery for up to 110 minutes in places without an outlet like balconies. A 6-in-1 station acts as control panel, charger, stabilizer and portable storage, and the robot is app-controllable. ECOVACS lists a 12-stage safety protection system including anti-fall measures.

Why We Like It

Watching a robot clean the outside of a window you can't reach is genuinely satisfying, and it handles the most-avoided chore in the house.

Highlights

  • 6-in-1 carrying/charging station
