Yoto Mini (2024 Edition) Kids Audio Player

About this product

Yoto Mini lets children play audio by inserting physical cards: insert a card to play, remove it to stop, and use the dials for volume and tracks, per the listing. The card library covers 1000+ audiobooks, music, educational content and more, plus Make Your Own cards. It has a pixel display and can also act as a sleep-sounds player, a bedtime storyteller, an OK-to-wake clock or a portable speaker, and it works with headphones. Battery life is up to 14 hours. It has no camera, no microphone and no ads, and parents can control it through a free app.

Why We Like It

It gives kids independence over what they listen to without handing them a screen, which is why parents swear by it for car rides and bedtime.
