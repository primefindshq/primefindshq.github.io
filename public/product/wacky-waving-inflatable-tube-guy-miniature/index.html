Wacky Waving Inflatable Tube Guy: The Original Miniature Edition (RP Minis)

Check price 4.0(44,792)

About this product

The roadside tube dancer, now on your desk. This is the miniature edition of the Wacky Waving Inflatable Tube Guy: a 17-inch fabric figure on a small black base with a fan inside, which the listing says makes him dance, wiggle and shake just like the real thing. It comes with a 32-page mini book about the larger-than-life origins of the flailing arm man, written by Conor Riordan and illustrated by Gemma Correll. Power is the catch: the listing says it runs on a 9-volt battery or on a specific AC adapter (100-240 V input, 9 V DC output at 1 A or 2 A), and that neither the battery nor the adapter is included. The listing recommends a rechargeable Ni-MH or a new alkaline battery, warns against Carbon-Zinc batteries, and suggests short runs of 5 to 10 minutes. The box is 3.75 by 3 by 1.88 inches. The listing pitches it for a desk, dorm room or bedside table and as a gift for birthdays, white elephant parties and holidays.

Why We Like It

It takes one of the most ridiculous sights in advertising and puts it beside your keyboard. Flip the switch and a tiny red figure flails, flops and bounces back up, exactly as unserious as it sounds.
