oneisall 7-in-1 Dog Grooming Vacuum Kit, Blue

$84.99 4.6(12,315)

About this product

This oneisall kit connects grooming tools to a vacuum so loose hair is sucked straight into a 1.5 L detachable dust cup while you groom, per the listing. It includes a grooming brush, a deshedding tool, an electric clipper, a paw trimmer and a nail grinder among its 7 tools. It has 3 suction levels and runs at about 59 dB. The hose is 5.2 ft and the power cord 8.7 ft, and accessories store in an included bag. It's rated for 110-130 V. This is the blue model LM2.

Why We Like It

Grooming a shedding dog at home usually means fur everywhere; this pulls the hair away as you go, so cleanup is emptying a cup.
