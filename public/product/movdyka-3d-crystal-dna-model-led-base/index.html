Movdyka 3D Crystal DNA Model with Color-Changing LED Base

Check price 4.7(321)

About this product

A double helix floating inside glass. The Movdyka DNA model is a clear K9 crystal block with a 3D double-helix pattern laser-engraved inside it, so the pattern sits within the crystal without breaking its surface. It stands on a black plastic LED base with a touch switch and 6 light modes: the color can change automatically or stay on a fixed hue. The base runs on 3 AAA batteries or from a USB cable, which the listing says connects to a computer, car charger or power bank. The crystal measures 50 by 50 by 80 mm and weighs 0.52 kg; the base measures 68 by 68 by 28 mm. The listing's contents are the crystal, LED base, data cable, cleaning cloth, gift card and gift box. It is pitched as a gift for science teachers, biology lovers and graduates, and as desk decor or a night light; the listing mentions birthdays, Christmas, graduation and retirement, and also calls it a paperweight.

Why We Like It

It is a piece of desk sculpture with a clear subject: a glowing double helix that changes color on its own. Compact enough for a shelf or workstation, and it comes boxed with a gift card.

Highlights

  • 3D double helix laser-engraved inside a clear K9 crystal
  • Black LED base with 6 light modes and a touch switch
  • Runs on 3 AAA batteries or a USB cable
  • Crystal 50 x 50 x 80 mm, base 68 x 68 x 28 mm
  • Comes with cleaning cloth, gift card and gift box
