Velieta 17" Cyberpunk Beverage Refrigerator with LED Light-Up Glass Door

Check price 4.4(328)

About this product

A fridge that looks like a gaming-room prop. The Velieta Cyberpunk Beverage Refrigerator has an illuminated glass door with a cyberpunk light pattern, so the drinks inside become part of the display. It is a freestanding, single-door compressor unit with 3.0 cubic feet of space that the listing says holds up to 128 standard 12 oz cans, arranged on 4 removable wire shelves that you can rearrange for cans or tall bottles. The temperature is adjustable from 34°F to 64°F with digital touch controls, and it has a built-in child safety lock with two keys. It stands 31.29 inches tall, 17.51 wide and 18.89 deep, in matte black, and it is frost free. It runs on 115 volts with a North American plug; the listing notes it may need a converter outside the United States. The listing gives a 1-year warranty and pitches it for gaming rooms, home bars, bedrooms, kitchens and offices.

Why We Like It

Most beverage fridges disappear into a room; this one is the centerpiece. The door glows in blue, cyan and orange circuit lines, so a shelf of cans turns into a backdrop for a gaming setup or home bar.

Highlights

  • 34°F to 64°F adjustable range with digital touch controls
  • Child safety lock with 2 keys
  • Freestanding, 17.51 x 18.89 x 31.29 inches; 115 V
