Ninja CREAMi 7-in-1 Ice Cream Maker (NC299AMZ)

Check price 4.5(4,248)

About this product

Freeze it. Spin it. Turn it into dessert. The Ninja CREAMi is not a blender and not a traditional churn-as-it-freezes ice cream maker: you prepare a base, freeze it in the pint for 24 hours, and the machine's Creamify Technology shaves through the uniformly frozen pint to turn it into a smooth, scoopable frozen dessert in minutes. It has 7 one-touch programs: Ice Cream, Gelato, Sorbet, Milkshake, Smoothie Bowl, Lite Ice Cream and Mix-In. The Mix-In program spreads chocolate chips, candy, nuts, fruit or cookies through the finished pint, and a Re-spin function lets you process the pint a second time for a softer texture. The listing describes making desserts from ingredients you choose, including dairy-free and vegan bases. This is the 1-pint set (Matte Black, 800W, 120V/60Hz), listed as including one 16 oz CREAMi pint with storage lid, the Creamerizer Paddle, the outer bowl with lid, and a recipe guide with 30 recipes. Pints, storage lids and the paddle are listed as top-rack dishwasher safe. Pint compatibility, per the listing: NC299 and NC300 series pints only. It measures about 12.07 x 6.52 x 15.95 inches and weighs about 13 lb.

Why We Like It

It changes what a freezer is for. A pint of frozen base goes in, and a few minutes later it comes out as whatever you picked: ice cream, sorbet, a shake, or a smoothie bowl. It's a playful machine that turns a simple base into something you customize, and an easy gift for someone who likes making things at home.

Highlights

  • 7 one-touch programs: Ice Cream, Gelato, Sorbet, Milkshake, Smoothie Bowl, Lite Ice Cream, Mix-In
  • Creamify Technology; Re-spin function
  • Base is frozen for 24 hours, then processed in minutes
  • Includes one 16 oz pint with lid, Creamerizer Paddle, outer bowl, 30-recipe guide
  • Pints, lids and paddle top-rack dishwasher safe; NC299/NC300 series pints only
