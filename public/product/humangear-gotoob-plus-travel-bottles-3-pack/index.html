About this product

GoToob+ bottles are soft, squeezable travel tubes for shampoo, lotion and other liquids. Each small bottle holds 1.7 fl oz, a TSA carry-on friendly size, per the listing. The LoopLock collar twists to lock the cap closed, the contoured tube stands on its own for filling and cleaning, a textured collar area can be written on to label contents, and the loop is carabiner compatible. They're made from food-safe, BPA-free and phthalate-free material. This 3-pack is clear, green and blue.
