Wood Trick Cyber Hand 3D Wooden Puzzle

Check price 4.7(92)

About this product

You build it, then you wear it. The Wood Trick Cyber Hand is a 586-piece wooden mechanical model that assembles, without glue, into a robotic-looking hand with moving joints. Once built, it fits over your hand with slots for your fingers, so you can perform handshakes, high-fives, thumbs-up and other gestures, or use it as a stand for a phone or tablet. The listing lists dimensions of 5.43 x 18.3 x 6.77 in and estimates about 8 hours to assemble, describing it as suited to experienced puzzle builders. It comes with an illustrated step-by-step guide.

Why We Like It

Most 3D wooden puzzles end up as static display pieces. This one becomes a wearable, moving mechanism you control with your own hand, which makes the build itself feel like the whole point rather than a means to a shelf object.

  • Performs gestures like handshakes, high-fives, thumbs-up
  • Can double as a phone or tablet stand
  • About 8 hours to assemble; illustrated guide included
