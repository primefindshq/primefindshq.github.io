About this product

Orboot Earth is a physical globe paired with a free companion app: hold a phone or tablet camera up to it and augmented-reality animals, landmarks and cultural details appear on screen, with over 1,000 facts across the listing's description. It includes interactive quizzes and games built around geography and memory, and a paper passport for collecting stickers as you "travel" to different places on the globe. The listing lists it for ages 4-10 and positions it for both home and classroom use. No screen is built into the globe itself; the AR runs through your own device's camera in the app.

Why We Like It

It turns a static globe into something you point a camera at and get a small AR show out of, which makes geography feel like exploration instead of a lesson. A distinctive, well-reviewed gift for a curious kid.
