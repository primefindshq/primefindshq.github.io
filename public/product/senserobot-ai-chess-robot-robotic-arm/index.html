About this product

Most chess apps stop at a screen. This one reaches across the board: a robotic arm picks up and moves real chess pieces while you play, so a game against the AI feels like sitting across from an actual opponent. It offers 25 AI difficulty levels from first-timer to advanced, plus 1,200+ exercises, endgame training, classic game replay, hints, and undos. Voice coaching guides the practice, and the companion app adds Lichess play, PGN export, progress tracking, and multiple player profiles, with Wi-Fi OTA updates to keep features current. Because a physical arm does the moving, pieces travel at a natural, controlled pace rather than screen-speed animation, and the connected features need Wi-Fi and app setup.

Why We Like It

It turns chess back into a tabletop object. A robot arm that reaches out, lifts a knight, and sets it down is the kind of thing people stop mid-conversation to watch, and it happens to be a genuinely capable training partner underneath.
