About this product

The Tumbler replaces whetstones with a two-part system. A magnetic angle support holds the knife at a fixed 15° or 20° angle, and you roll the handheld sharpener along the edge: one side is a diamond-plated disc for sharpening, the other a stainless-steel helix disc for honing, per the listing. It's designed for kitchen, chef, field and utility knives. This is the wood version, weighing about 1.4 lb.

Why We Like It

It makes sharpening feel like a satisfying two-minute ritual instead of a skill you have to learn, and the wood-and-steel set looks good enough to leave out.

Highlights

  • Magnetic angle block: 15° and 20°
  • Diamond-plated sharpening disc
  • Stainless-steel helix honing disc
  • No whetstone or water needed
  • Wood finish, ~1.4 lb
