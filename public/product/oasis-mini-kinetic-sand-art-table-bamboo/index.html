Oasis Mini Kinetic Sand Art Table, Bamboo

Check price 4.4(397)

About this product

A desk piece that draws itself. The Oasis Mini is a kinetic sand art table: it slowly carves patterns into a bed of sand, and you choose what it draws from the app, on iOS or Android, over Wi-Fi. The library holds hundreds of patterns, and you can also make your own with the included tools and tutorials. The app also controls the RGB lighting (100+ light modes), the drawing speed, and playlists. It is built with real glass and bamboo, measures about 9 x 9 x 3 inches, and the listing pitches it as a calming, mindful desk or living-room piece and as a gift. The box includes the Oasis Mini, a sand packet, a ball bearing, a sand smoothing card, a user manual, a power adapter, and a USB power cable.

Why We Like It

It is a piece of moving art rather than a gadget: a slow, hypnotic drawing that keeps redrawing itself in sand, and it looks good on a desk when nothing is happening on it.

Highlights

  • App control on iOS and Android over Wi-Fi: patterns, speed, playlists, and RGB lighting
  • 100+ light modes
  • Real glass and bamboo construction, about 9 x 9 x 3 inches
  • Includes sand, ball bearing, smoothing card, manual, power adapter, and USB cable
