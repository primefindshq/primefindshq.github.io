RayCue Maclock WB-8 Retro Pixel Alarm Clock

Check price 4.5(97)

About this product

A tiny vintage computer that happens to be a clock. The RayCue Maclock WB-8 is shaped like an early-1980s all-in-one PC, with a high-contrast pixel LCD that shows time, calendar, day and room temperature together, plus a hidden pixel smiley animation at startup. There are five alarm modes (off, one-time, weekdays, weekends, daily) set with a top touch dial, and a bottom dial for brightness. The listing includes a floppy-disk-shaped prop, retro stickers, a USB-C cable and a manual; inserting the floppy-disk sticker triggers a mock boot animation. The listing also notes the temperature reading pauses briefly while the backlight is on, to avoid heat interference, and recommends turning the backlight off for an accurate reading.

Why We Like It

It miniaturizes a whole era of computer design into a clock small enough to sit next to a keyboard, complete with a fake boot-up sequence. A genuinely funny, well-made desk object for anyone who grew up around or loves that look.

Highlights

  • Shows time, calendar, day and room temperature at once
  • 5 alarm modes; adjustable brightness
  • Floppy-disk prop triggers a mock boot animation
  • USB-C powered; cable, stickers and manual included
