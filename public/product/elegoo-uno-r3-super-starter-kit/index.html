About this product

ELEGOO's Super Starter Kit includes the UNO R3 controller, an LCD1602 with pre-soldered header, a breadboard power module, ultrasonic and DHT11 sensors, a joystick, IR receiver and remote, SG90 servo, stepper motor, relay and DC motor with fan blade, per the listing.
