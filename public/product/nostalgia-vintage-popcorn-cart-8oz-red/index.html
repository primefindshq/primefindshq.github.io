About this product

This Nostalgia popcorn cart recreates a vintage movie-theater popcorn machine as a standalone cart. Its stainless-steel kettle holds 8 oz of kernels, has a built-in stirring system and a dual-hinged lid, and pops up to 32 cups per batch, per the listing. The kettle removes for cleaning. The windows are tempered safety glass, and the interior is lighted. It works with Nostalgia popcorn kits. The cart stands 53 inches tall (model CCP510). This listing is the red color.

It turns a basement or living room into a home theater with one object, and the smell sells it before anyone sees it. A show-stopper for movie-night families.
