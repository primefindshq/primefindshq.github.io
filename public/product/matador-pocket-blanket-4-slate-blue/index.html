About this product

The Pocket Blanket 4.0 opens to 63 x 44 in. (seats 2-4, per the listing) and packs down to 4.5 x 2.7 x 1.1 in. in an attached storage pouch. It's made from ripstop fabric with a waterproof coating, and has integrated stakes plus corner sand pockets to hold it down in wind.
