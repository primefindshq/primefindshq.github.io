Heated Ice Cream Scoop, Rechargeable

Check price 4.6(38)

About this product

This scoop heats its zinc-alloy head to one of 3 levels (120°F/140°F/160°F, per the listing) so it moves through hard ice cream, gelato or sorbet without waiting for it to soften. An LED display shows the heat setting and battery status, and the listing says it reaches its highest temperature in about 90 seconds. It's cordless, powered by a built-in 2500mAh battery that recharges over USB, and has a 10-minute auto shutoff. The listing lists a 2.05 oz scoop capacity, a non-stick head, an IP67-rated waterproof body, and a drip-proof stand.

Why We Like It

It solves the specific annoyance of a scoop that won't go through frozen-solid ice cream, and does it with a heated head instead of the usual run-it-under-hot-water routine. A small, satisfying kitchen gadget and an easy gift.

Highlights

  • LED display for heat and battery status
  • Cordless; built-in 2500mAh battery, USB recharging
  • 10-minute auto shutoff; IP67-rated waterproof body
  • 2.05 oz scoop capacity; drip-proof stand
