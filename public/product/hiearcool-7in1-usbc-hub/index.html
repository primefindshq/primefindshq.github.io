Connect more, carry less. This 7-in-1 USB-C hub adds an HDMI output (up to 4K@30Hz), two USB 3.0 ports (5Gbps), 100W USB-C power delivery pass-through, and both SD and TF card slots to a single cable — everything a modern laptop stripped down to.
