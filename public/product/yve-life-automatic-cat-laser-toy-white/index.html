YVE LIFE Automatic Cat Laser Toy, White

$19.94 4.3(10,936)

About this product

This YVE LIFE toy projects a laser dot that moves in random patterns driven by two motors. A sensor switches it on when it detects your pet moving, and each session runs 15 minutes before turning off; the sensor can re-trigger every 2 hours, per the listing. It has fast, slow and mixed speed modes, and the laser head angle adjusts up to 50° vertically. A 1200 mAh USB-rechargeable battery charges in about 2.5 hours and lasts up to 2 days, or it can run plugged in. The listing recommends placing it 8-35 inches high.

Why We Like It

It plays with the cat when you can't, and the random pattern keeps it from becoming predictable. A cheap fix for an indoor cat with too much energy.
