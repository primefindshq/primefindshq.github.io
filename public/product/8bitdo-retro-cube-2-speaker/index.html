8BitDo Retro Cube 2 Speaker

Check price 4.6(120)

About this product

The 8BitDo Retro Cube 2 takes the look of a classic console and turns it into a portable Bluetooth speaker, with dual drivers for stereo sound and D-pad-style controls on top. It connects over Bluetooth 5.3, 2.4G wireless or wired USB, switches between Music and Gaming sound modes with one press, and has an integrated wireless charging dock built into the base. The listing lists a 2000mAh battery, a built-in microphone, and a USB-C adapter included, and positions it for a desk, travel or gaming setup.

Why We Like It

It's a speaker that looks like it belongs next to a retro console rather than a generic Bluetooth speaker shape, and the wireless charging dock built into the base means it can double as a phone stand between uses.

Highlights

  • Bluetooth 5.3, 2.4G wireless or wired USB connections
