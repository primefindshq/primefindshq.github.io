Divoom Times Gate, Cyberpunk 5-Screen Pixel Art Desk Display

Check price 4.4(909)

About this product

Five screens, one cyberpunk gadget. The Divoom Times Gate is a digital desk display in a silver, art-deco-styled metal frame, built around five full-color LCD screens that each show 128x128 pixel art or animated GIFs. It connects over 2.4GHz Wi-Fi, not Bluetooth, and the listing says it has no built-in battery: it needs continuous USB power and is designed to stay on the desk rather than travel. Through the Divoom app you can display world clocks, real-time weather and holiday calendars, plus live data such as stock prices, social media follower counts and game stats. You can also create your own pixel art or pull from a community gallery of shared designs that updates daily, and personalize the display with animations, text and graphics. The listing describes it as a visual display, not built for touch input or audio output. Product size is listed as 283 x 47 x 97 mm, and it weighs 2 pounds.

Why We Like It

Five little screens turn into a control panel for your desk: weather on one, a stock ticker on another, and pixel art wherever you want it. It looks like it walked off a cyberpunk set and updates itself over Wi-Fi.

Highlights

  • Five full-color 128x128 LCD screens for pixel art and animated GIFs
  • 2.4GHz Wi-Fi control through the Divoom app, no Bluetooth
  • Displays world clocks, weather, holidays, stocks, social stats and game stats
  • Create your own pixel art or use daily community-shared designs
  • USB powered, no built-in battery; 283 x 47 x 97 mm, 2 lb
