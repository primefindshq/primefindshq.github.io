FiayaCom Bluetooth Retro Rotary Phone

Check price 4.5(23)

About this product

This is a rotary-dial phone body wired for a modern phone: it pairs to your smartphone over Bluetooth so you can make and receive calls through it, hands-free, or use it as a Bluetooth speaker for music. There are no phone cords to run, since it charges over USB and runs cordless, and the listing says it reconnects automatically when you're back in range. Holding the * key activates your phone's voice assistant through the handset, for things like placing a call or setting an alarm. It's finished in black with gold trim, at about 6 x 6 x 10 in.

Why We Like It

It looks like a prop from an old film and works like a real accessory: a rotary phone on the desk that actually rings your calls and plays your music over Bluetooth. A genuinely surprising piece of desk decor.

Highlights

  • Hands-free speakerphone; USB rechargeable, cordless
  • Reconnects automatically when back in range (listed)
