ROKR Magic Piano 3D Wooden Puzzle Music Box, 223 Pieces, 1:20 Scale

Check price 4.6(988)

About this product

Build it, then watch it play. The ROKR Magic Piano is a 1:20 scale wooden grand piano kit, 223 pieces of laser-cut wood joined by mortise-and-tenon joints, so no glue is required. The listing puts the build at about 4.5 hours. Its base is styled like a book, and the transparent structure shows off the gears inside. Once it is finished you wind the spring key under the book, and gear transmission drives the keys to play the melody "Variations on the Canon" by themselves, with no batteries. The lid folds open and closed, a prop stick holds it up, and a key cover lowers to protect the keys. The kit comes with a step-by-step manual, pre-cut wood sheets, a music movement, wax and sandpaper. It is listed for ages 14 and up, carries a warning not to swallow small parts, and is pitched as a gift and as desk or shelf decor for home or office.

Why We Like It

Two rewards in one: the slow, satisfying build, and then a display piece that actually performs. Wind the key and the keys play on their own while you watch the gears turn through the transparent case.

  • Folding lid, prop stick, key cover and see-through gear structure
