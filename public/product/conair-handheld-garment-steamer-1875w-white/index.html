About this product

This Conair handheld steamer uses 1875 watts to remove wrinkles from clothing, curtains and upholstery. According to the listing, it heats up in about 35 seconds and a 7.3 oz removable water tank gives up to 20 minutes of continuous steam. A heated quilted-aluminum soleplate and built-in creaser allow pressed finishes on collars and cuffs without an ironing board. It has multiple steam settings, an 8-foot cord, and three attachments: a silicone band, a bristle brush and a delicate-fabric spacer. This is the white model GS59L.

Why We Like It

It's faster than setting up an ironing board and works on things an iron can't reach, like hanging curtains. The listing pitches it for travel too.
