ULANZI VL119 RGB LED Light Wand

Check price 4.4(1,379)

About this product

The ULANZI VL119 is a 50 cm handheld tube light for video and photos, built around 114 LEDs with a CRI95+ rating for natural-looking light. The listing lists a full 0-360° hue range, 0-100% dimming, color temperature adjustable from 2500K to 9000K, and 20 preset light effects, all read on a built-in LCD display. It runs on a 3300mAh battery, listed at about 70 minutes of continuous use at full power, charges over USB-C in about 2 hours, and can be used while charging. A standard 1/4-inch mount lets it attach to a camera, tripod or light stand. It weighs about 205 g.

Why We Like It

It's a compact, controllable light source for portraits, streams or product shots, useful well beyond one specific use, and small enough to keep in a bag rather than a light-stand and softbox setup.

Highlights

  • 114 LEDs, CRI95+, full RGB hue and 2500K-9000K color temp
  • 0-100% dimmable; 20 preset light effects
  • 3300mAh battery, ~70 min runtime; USB-C charging
  • Built-in LCD for settings; usable while charging
  • Standard 1/4-inch mount; about 205 g
