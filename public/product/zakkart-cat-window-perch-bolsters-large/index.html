Zakkart Cat Window Perch with Bolsters, Large

$23.99 4.7(5,922)

About this product

The Zakkart perch hooks onto a windowsill (the window needs a slot at least 0.5 in. deep) and adjusts to sills up to 9 in. deep, with no suction cups or drilling, per the listing. The frame is hardwood and metal, it holds up to 40 lb, and the large bed is 24 x 15 in. with bolsters. The cushion is sewn onto the frame with a sleeve and is removable for machine washing. This is the natural wood/gray large size.

Why We Like It

Cats love a window seat, and this gives them one without taking floor space or trusting suction cups. It moves easily between windows.
