Wingspan Board Game (Stonemaier Games)

$55.00 4.8(12,845)

About this product

Wingspan is a strategy game where players attract birds to their wildlife preserves, gaining food from custom dice in a birdfeeder dice tower, laying egg miniatures and playing from 170 unique bird cards whose abilities chain together, per the listing. It won the 2019 Kennerspiel des Jahres. It plays 1-5 players in 60-90 minutes, with about 6 minutes of setup. Components include 170 bird cards, 26 bonus cards, 8 goal tiles, 103 food tokens, 75 egg miniatures and 5 wooden dice.

Why We Like It

It's the board game people buy for its beauty and keep playing for the strategy. The bird art and dice tower make it a centerpiece on the table.
