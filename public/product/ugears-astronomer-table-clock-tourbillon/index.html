About this product

The Astronomer is a UGEARS 3D wooden puzzle that builds into a fully working table clock. It includes 338 wooden parts and instructions, and the pieces fit together without glue or special tools, per the listing. Its centerpiece is a tourbillon, a rotating escapement mechanism shown in motion on the front of the clock. It's listed for adult builders.

Why We Like It

It's a long, satisfying build that ends as a clock you can watch tick, with the tourbillon turning in plain sight. A good gift for anyone who likes mechanisms more than screens.
