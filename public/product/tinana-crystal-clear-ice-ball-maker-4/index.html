TINANA Crystal Clear Ice Ball Maker, 4 Spheres

$39.99 4.4(1,446)

About this product

The TINANA ice ball maker produces four large, clear ice spheres per batch, each about 2.36 in. across, per the listing. Fill it with water and freeze it for 26-30 hours, then remove the tray to get nearly transparent spheres. Large spheres melt more slowly than regular cubes and fit standard rocks glasses. The mold is made of silicone for easy release.

Why We Like It

Clear ice is the bar trick that makes a home drink look professional, and the patience it takes makes every sphere feel earned.
