YOSUKATA 14" Carbon Steel Wok, Round Bottom

About this product

YOSUKATA's 14-inch wok is made from heavy-duty black carbon steel and pre-seasoned at over 600°F, though the listing notes it should be seasoned with oil before first use. It has an ergonomic wooden handle and about 5.8 liters of capacity, and works for stir-frying, pan-frying, braising, deep-frying, steaming and smoking. The round bottom is best suited to gas stovetops and open fire, and a care and seasoning manual is included.

Why We Like It

A proper carbon steel wok gets hot enough for real stir-fry flavor, and it only gets better with use.
