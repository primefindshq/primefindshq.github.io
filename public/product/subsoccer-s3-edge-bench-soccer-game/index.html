SUBSOCCER S3 Edge Bench Soccer Game

About this product

Soccer. But you never leave the bench. The SUBSOCCER S3 Edge is a portable bench soccer set: two enclosed benches face each other across a netted playing table, and each player controls the ball with their feet from a seated position to score into the opponent's net. The listing describes this as the next-generation version of the original S3, now with a dedicated gaming mat included, redesigned bench legs for added stability, and an anthracite grey finish over a powder-coated steel frame. It sets up with the included tools in minutes and packs away for home, office, beach or backyard use, and each bench is listed to support up to 150 kg. The listing recommends it for players aged 6 and up. Listed contents: the table, 2 benches with nets, an official SUBSOCCER game ball, assembly tools and a user manual. Listed dimensions are 15.74 x 7.67 x 47.24 in at about 40 lb; check the current listing if you need the assembled play footprint, since it isn't spelled out separately. As with any active game, keep an eye on how it's used and follow the included manual's setup and safety guidance.

Why We Like It

It's an easy game to understand and a strange one to picture until you see it: two people, seated, playing soccer with their feet across a table-sized pitch. It turns a spare bit of floor into a fast, competitive game that doesn't need a screen.
