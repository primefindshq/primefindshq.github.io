About this product

The DREO heater uses a 1500 W PTC ceramic element with a heat-funnel design to spread warm air, per the listing. A thermostat sets 41-95°F in 1°F increments. It includes tilt-detection tip-over protection, overheat protection, V0 flame-retardant materials and a safety plug. A brushless DC motor keeps it to about 34 dB. It has Power, ECO and Fan Only modes, a remote, a 12-hour timer, mute, child lock and a carry handle. This is the gold 10-inch model.

Why We Like It

A quiet, precise heater under a desk or in a bedroom means you can keep the whole house cooler, and the remote means not getting up to adjust it.
