About this product

The Retevis RT628 is a two-pack of compact walkie talkies designed for kids ages 6-12. They have 22 channels, a VOX mode for hands-free talking without pressing buttons, 8 volume levels and a keypad lock to prevent accidental channel changes, per the listing. They're license-free, have belt clips, and each measures 6.2 x 2.1 x 1.1 in. Each unit needs 3 AA batteries (not included). This is the red pair.
