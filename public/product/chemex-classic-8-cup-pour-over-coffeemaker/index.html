Chemex Classic 8-Cup Pour-Over Coffeemaker

$48.95 4.8(8,732)

About this product

The Chemex Classic is the hourglass-shaped glass pour-over coffeemaker with a wooden collar and leather tie. It's made of non-porous borosilicate glass that won't absorb odors or chemical residues, per the listing. This is the 8-cup size (Chemex measures a cup as 5 oz), with a 40 fl oz capacity. Brewed coffee can be covered and refrigerated for reheating. It uses Chemex bonded filters (FP-1, FC-100, FS-100 or FSU-100), which are not included.

Why We Like It

A coffee maker that's been in design museums and still looks better on a counter than almost anything else. It makes a clean, bright cup for the whole table.
