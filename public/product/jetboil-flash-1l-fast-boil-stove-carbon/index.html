About this product

The Jetboil Flash is an integrated camp stove and 1-liter insulated cooking cup. A turn-and-click igniter lights the burner without a match, and the listing gives a boil time of 16 oz of water in about 2 minutes. The cup has a color-coded, rubberized safe-touch grip, an insulated cozy, and a three-point locking connection to the burner. The stove and a 100 g JetPower fuel canister (sold separately) pack inside the cup, and the bottom cup doubles as a measuring cup or bowl. It weighs 13.12 oz. This is the carbon color.

Why We Like It

Coffee or a hot meal in two minutes anywhere, from a single object that packs into itself. It's the camping upgrade people rave about after the first trip.
