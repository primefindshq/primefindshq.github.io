About this product

This GoSports set includes two regulation-size 4 x 2 ft boards with a printed barnwood design on steel frames, eight all-weather regulation bean bags (6 x 6 in., 1 lb each; 4 red, 4 blue), a carrying case and game rules, per the listing.
