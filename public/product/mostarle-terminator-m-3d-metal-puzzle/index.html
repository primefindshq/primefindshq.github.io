About this product

This is a build-it-yourself robot made of metal: a Mostarle 3D metal puzzle in a sci-fi mechanical design. Once assembled, the Terminator M has fully adjustable body joints, so it can stand, crouch, or hold a weapon, and you can set it into whatever pose you like. It has an LED light feature that you can turn on or off. Long-press the switch while the light is on to cycle its brightness from dim to bright and back, or double-tap it for a breathing-light mode where the glow fades in and out, and the listing describes three light color options. The listing gives its size as about 8 inches, its material as metal, and pitches it as a gift for adults and teenagers. The listing does not state a piece count for this model.

Why We Like It

Most desk figures arrive finished. This one is the project: you assemble a poseable metal robot piece by piece, and you end up with a lit, articulated sci-fi figure that you built yourself.
