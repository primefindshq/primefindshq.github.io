Questions people ask

What is a good gift for dad?

Something he'll use every week: a MEATER Plus or TempPro meat thermometer for the grill, a NOCO Boost jump starter or AstroAI tire inflator for the car, a Klein 11-in-1 screwdriver for the toolbox, or a YETI Rambler tumbler for his coffee.

What can I get my dad for under $25?

The TempPro instant-read thermometer, the Weber chimney starter, the Cuisinart grill press, a digital tire pressure gauge, the FORTEM trunk organizer, the DEWALT 40-piece bit set, the Klein screwdriver, an infrared thermometer, a Victorinox Swiss Army knife or a Carhartt beanie were all under $25 when we last checked.

What do you get a dad who has everything?

Upgrades to things he already does: a wire-free MEATER Plus probe instead of an old dial thermometer, a code reader so he can see why the check engine light is on, a slim Ridge wallet, or an AeroPress for better coffee.

Are these prices guaranteed?

No. Amazon prices change often, especially around Black Friday and Christmas. Each pick shows the price we last checked; the current price is on Amazon.
