Questions people ask

What's the best tech gift under $50?

Soundcore P20i earbuds, the Soundcore Q20i ANC headphones, a Fire TV Stick 4K Plus or a 3D printing pen are all strong under-$50 tech gifts.

Which headphones make the best gift?

For noise cancelling, the Bose QuietComfort headphones and AirPods Pro 3 are the headline picks. For runners, the SHOKZ OpenRun Pro bone-conduction headphones leave ears open.

A Kindle Paperwhite, an Echo Dot, a Fire TV Stick or a portable Bluetooth speaker are simple to set up and use.
