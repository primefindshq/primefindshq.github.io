Questions people ask

What do you get a coffee lover who has everything?

A heated mug like the Ember Mug 2, a quality burr grinder, or a new brewing method they haven't tried yet (pour-over, AeroPress or cold brew).

What's a good coffee gift under $25?

A handheld milk frother, an iced coffee sleeve, a pour-over dripper or an insulated coffee tumbler are all under $25 here.

Is a burr grinder a good gift?

Yes, for anyone who buys whole beans. A burr grinder gives a more even grind than a blade grinder, which is why coffee fans often call it the best upgrade.
