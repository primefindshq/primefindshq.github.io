Questions people ask

What is a good gift for mom?

Something cozy or something she uses every day: a heated throw or fleece blanket, an Ember temperature-control mug, satin pillowcases, the Revlon One-Step hair dryer brush or a rechargeable neck reading light are all on this list.

What can I get my mom for under $25?

Satin pillowcases, a milk frother, the neck reading light, a LEGO mini orchid, a mug warmer, eos body lotion, heatless curls or under-eye patches all cost under $25 when we last checked.

What do you get a mom who has everything?

Small upgrades to things she already does: a mug warmer for the coffee she forgets, a glass teapot with an infuser for loose tea, an ultrasonic jewelry cleaner, or LEGO flowers that never need watering.

Are these prices guaranteed?

No. Amazon prices change often, especially around Black Friday and Christmas. Each pick shows the price we last checked; the current price is on Amazon.
