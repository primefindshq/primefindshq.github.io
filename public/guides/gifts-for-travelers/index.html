Questions people ask

What's the best gift for someone who travels a lot?

Noise-cancelling headphones, an AirTag for their bag, a high-capacity power bank and a good sleep mask cover the most common travel pain points.

What's a good travel gift under $30?

A Repel travel umbrella, a digital luggage scale, a Victorinox Swiss Army knife, a neck wallet, or an AirTag holder.

What should a traveler not be gifted?

Big or heavy items. Travelers value things that pack small, so stick to compact gear.
