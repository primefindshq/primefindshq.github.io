Questions people ask

When is Amazon Black Friday 2026?

Black Friday is Friday, November 27, 2026. Amazon typically starts its Black Friday deals several days earlier and runs them through Cyber Monday, but it announces the exact dates itself, so check Amazon's deals page.

Are the prices on this page Black Friday prices?

No. They're the regular prices we last checked on Amazon US, so you have a baseline. If the price on Amazon is lower on the day, that's the deal.

How were these products picked?

Every pick is one of the most-reviewed items in our catalog, almost all with 100,000+ ratings and a 4.4 star average or better. Popular products are the ones Amazon most often discounts for big sales.
