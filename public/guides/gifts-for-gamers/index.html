Questions people ask

What is a good gift for a gamer who has everything?

Upgrades to gear they use every day: a pro-level controller, a wireless gaming mouse, a headset, or a desk lighting kit. A Stream Deck is a favorite for gamers who also stream.

What gaming gifts work on any platform?

Headsets with a 3.5mm or USB connection, mouse pads, RGB light strips and floor lamps and desk gear work regardless of console.

Is the Nintendo Switch 2 or PS5 a better gift?

It depends on who it's for. The Switch 2 is portable and strong for family and Nintendo games; the PS5 is a home console aimed at big third-party releases. Check which one their friends play on.
