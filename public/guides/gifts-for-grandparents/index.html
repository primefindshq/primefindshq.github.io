Questions people ask

What is a good gift for grandparents?

Something comfortable or easy to enjoy every day: a Sunbeam heated throw or a soft Bedsure fleece blanket, a Glocusent neck reading light, a window hummingbird feeder, or a 1000-piece puzzle with a rotating puzzle board.

What can I get my grandparents for under $25?

The Bedsure throw blanket, the Glocusent neck light, the Hiware glass teapot, the Oceanstar tea box, the Perky-Pet window feeder, the Buffalo Games puzzle and Kanoodle were all under $25 when we last checked.

What do you get grandparents who have everything?

Something tied to the family: a Canon Ivy 2 or Kodak Mini 2 pocket printer so photos of the grandkids become prints they can hold, or a shared hobby such as a puzzle you can finish together when you visit.

Are these prices guaranteed?

No. Amazon prices change often, especially around Black Friday and Christmas. Each pick shows the price we last checked; the current price is on Amazon.
