Questions people ask

What should I get a teenager who has everything?

Experiences they can hold: an instant camera with film, a room lighting upgrade like LED strips, or a speaker they can take to friends' houses. Consumables like film packs, lip masks and skincare sets are also safe because they get used up.

What's a good gift for a teen under $25?

Heatless curl sets, LED strip lights, a lip sleeping mask, a Squishmallows mystery pack or an iced-coffee sleeve are all under $25 on this list.

Are these gifts good for both teen boys and girls?

Most of them are. Earbuds, speakers, cameras, controllers, tumblers and room lighting are popular with every teen. The beauty picks lean toward teens who are into skincare and makeup.
