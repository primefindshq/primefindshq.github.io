Questions people ask

What beauty products make the best gifts?

Lip care (LANEIGE Lip Sleeping Mask), body products (Sol de Janeiro), hair oils (Moroccanoil) and hair tools are safe because they don't depend on skin type.

What's a good beauty gift under $25?

LANEIGE Lip Sleeping Mask, COSRX Snail Mucin, BIODANCE collagen masks, Sol de Janeiro Bum Bum Cream or the Kitsch Grinch hair perfume duo.

Are K-beauty products good gifts?

Yes. K-beauty brands like COSRX, BIODANCE, medicube and LANEIGE are some of the most-requested beauty products right now.
